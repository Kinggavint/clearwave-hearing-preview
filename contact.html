Answers

Location and contact questions, answered

Where is Clearwave Hearing and Audiology located?

The office is at 5710 Oleander Drive, Suite 110, Wilmington, NC 28403, in the Military Cutoff Road corridor. It is a short drive from Landfall, Ogden, Wrightsville Beach, and Porters Neck, with surface parking at the entrance and step access to the suite.

What are your office hours?

The office is open Monday through Thursday from 8:00 to 5:00 and Friday from 8:00 to 3:00, and closed Saturday and Sunday. Calls to (910) 262-4478 are answered by our own staff during those hours, and voicemail is returned the next business morning.

How do I reach you outside office hours?

Leave a voicemail at (910) 262-4478 or use the contact form on this page, and the office will respond on the next business day. For sudden hearing loss, severe ear pain, or drainage, contact a physician or urgent care right away rather than waiting.

Can I get help with hearing aids I bought somewhere else?

Often yes. Many devices from other providers can be cleaned, repaired, and reprogrammed here. Call the office with the brand and model, and we will tell you honestly whether we can support it before you make the trip.
